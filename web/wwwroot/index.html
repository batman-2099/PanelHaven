      Library maintenance

      Scans and folder watching update availability. Missing means the file is absent from an accessible library; unavailable means its location cannot be safely accessed. Reconnect a library and scan again before removing anything. Source files are never deleted.

      Exact duplicates share the SHA-256 of their file contents, not just a title or cover. Groups can continue across pages. Present duplicates cannot be removed here.

      Metadata validation

      Smart rules

      Smart lists update automatically as metadata, your reading progress, and your private ratings change. Use 1–20 rules. Year uses 0–9999; unknown years do not match. My rating uses 0–5 (0 means unrated).

      Private list folders

      Organize your reading lists in one level of private folders. Deleting a folder only unfiles its lists.
